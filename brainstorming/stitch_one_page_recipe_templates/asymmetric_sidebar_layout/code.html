Miso-Glazed Black Cod

Chef's Notes

The secret to the buttery texture is the long marination time. Do not rush the 48-hour window. If using frozen cod, ensure it is completely thawed and patted dry before adding the marinade. The sugar in the miso will burn quickly under a broiler; watch closely for a deep mahogany color without charring. Pair with a crisp, dry sake or a light ginger-infused rice.

menu_book Method of Preparation

01

In a small saucepan, bring the sake and mirin to a boil over high heat. Boil for 20 seconds to evaporate the alcohol. Turn heat down to low, add the miso paste, and whisk until dissolved. Increase heat to medium and add sugar, whisking constantly until the sugar has completely dissolved. Remove from heat and let cool to room temperature.

02

Pat the cod fillets thoroughly dry with paper towels. Place the fillets in a non-reactive glass dish or a large resealable plastic bag. Pour the cooled miso marinade over the fish, ensuring each piece is completely coated. Cover or seal and refrigerate for at least 24 hours, but ideally 48 hours.

03

Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Lightly wipe off excess marinade from the fillets with your fingers (do not rinse). Heat a lightly oiled oven-proof skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the fish skin-side up for 2 minutes until the marinade begins to caramelize.

04

Flip the fillets over and transfer the skillet to the oven. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes, or until the fish is opaque and flakes easily. For a deeper glaze, turn on the broiler for the last 1-2 minutes, watching carefully to prevent burning. Serve immediately with steamed rice and pickled ginger.
