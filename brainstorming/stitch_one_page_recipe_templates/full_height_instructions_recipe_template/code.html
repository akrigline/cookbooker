Classic Rosemary Roast Chicken

Chef's Notes

For the crispiest skin possible, salt the chicken the night before and leave it uncovered in the refrigerator. This "dry brining" process draws out moisture and allows the heat to crisp the skin immediately. If the chicken browns too quickly, cover the breast area loosely with a piece of aluminum foil during the last 20 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole roasting chicken (4-5 lbs)
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
  • 4 sprigs fresh rosemary, chopped
  • 1 head garlic, halved crosswise
  • 1 large yellow onion, quartered
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 1 large lemon, halved
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine or chicken broth

Instructions

01

Pre-heat oven to 425°F (220°C). Remove the chicken from the refrigerator 30 minutes before roasting to bring to room temperature. Pat the skin extremely dry with paper towels.

02

Season the cavity with salt and pepper. Stuff with the garlic, lemon, and two sprigs of rosemary. Truss the legs with kitchen twine and tuck the wings under the body.

03

Rub the softened butter over the entire surface of the skin. Season generously with remaining salt, pepper, and chopped rosemary. Place onion quarters in the roasting pan.

04

Roast for 60 to 75 minutes, or until the juices run clear and a thermometer inserted into the thickest part of the thigh registers 165°F (74°C).

05

Remove from oven and transfer chicken to a cutting board. Tent loosely with foil and let rest for at least 15 minutes. Use the pan juices to create a light jus with wine or broth.
