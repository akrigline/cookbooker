Slow-Roasted Heirloom Tomato Galette

Chef's Notes

For the best results, ensure your butter is ice-cold when preparing the crust. If the tomatoes are particularly juicy, let them sit on paper towels for 15 minutes after slicing to prevent a soggy bottom. This recipe favors the deep, concentrated sweetness of late-summer heirlooms, but can be adapted with cherry tomatoes if needed.

Ingredients

1.5 cups all-purpose flour
1/2 tsp fine sea salt
1 stick unsalted butter, chilled
1/4 cup ice water
1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
3 large heirloom tomatoes
4 oz goat cheese, room temp
2 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
1 large egg (for wash)
Pantry Check
Coarse sea salt for finishing
Black peppercorns, freshly ground

Instructions

  1. 01

    Prepare Crust: Pulse flour and salt in a processor. Add cubed butter; pulse until pea-sized. Stream in ice water and vinegar until dough barely holds. Wrap and chill 1 hour.

  2. 02

    Prep Tomatoes: Slice heirlooms into 1/4-inch rounds. Lay on paper towels, sprinkle lightly with salt, and let drain. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).

  3. 03

    Assemble: Roll dough on parchment to a 12-inch circle. Spread goat cheese in center, leaving a 2-inch border. Arrange tomato slices over cheese; sprinkle thyme.

  4. 04

    Fold & Bake: Pleat dough edges over tomatoes. Brush crust with egg wash. Drizzle oil over center. Bake 35–40 min until crust is deep gold and tomatoes are slumped.
